# Bankrupt (id `bankrupt`)

A Monopoly-style property game for 2-6 players (`MAX_PLAYERS` in `packages/shared/src/games/bankrupt/types.ts`; 8 is a one-line change). Logic in `packages/shared/src/games/bankrupt`, UI in `apps/web/src/games/bankrupt`, art in `apps/web/src/assets/sprites/bankrupt` (made by `.claude/skills/pogo-pixel-art/examples/bk_art.py`).

## Rules

- Everyone starts with $1000, **+$50 per seat in turn order** (the later you play, the more you start with); the seat order is random.
- Roll **one die** and walk the ring. **A six rolls again**, with no penalty however often it comes. Passing or landing on Start pays the **salary** ($200). Your turn **ends by itself** once the landing is dealt with (there is no End turn button): build and sell _before_ you roll. Only the Auction corner waits for you (auction a space, or Skip).
- A free space: **buy** it, or **cancel**: when you could have paid for it, it goes to **auction** (everybody bids, the mover too; the auction **opens at 70 % of the price** (`AUCTION_MIN_PCT`, `openingBid`, rounded up to 5) and is **skipped entirely when nobody but the player who passed has the money for that** (the space stays free); **a player who cannot afford the space never starts an auction** (it just stays free); **15 seconds** to bid: the clock restarts with every bid and when it runs out everybody who has not bid steps out (`GameDefinition.phaseClockMs` / `timeoutMove`, a clock that runs whatever the room's turn timer says); a player who has no more money than the highest bid **steps out by themselves**, and the screen greys out the prices) unless the host switched **Auction unbought spaces** off in the lobby (`auctionOnDecline`, default on), then it simply stays free. A space somebody owns: pay **rent**. A whole **colour set** doubles the base rent and lets the owner build up to 3 buildings (house, shop, hotel) per space; rent then follows the space's table. The four **special spaces** (Harbor, Railway, Airport, Electricity) cannot be built on: their rent depends on how many of the four the owner has: **1 = $35, 2 = $70, 3 = $100, 4 = $140** each.
- **Jail**: you reach it by **landing on the Jail space** or with a luck card. You are asked **at once** (phase `jailAsk`): **pay $150** and you are free (your turn simply ends), or **Pass**: then your next **3 turns are skipped by themselves** (`jailSkip` events) and nothing is asked again; there is no way out meanwhile.
- **Luck** (`?`): a random card from the board's deck (cash, collect from / pay every player, move to a space, a bill per building, go to jail).
- **Auction corner**: land there and you may auction one of your own spaces (without buildings). The seller can **cancel** the auction; the highest bidder pays the seller.
- **Trade on the Auction corner** (`Trade` in `types.ts`, phase `trade`, `TradePanel.tsx`): landing on the corner starts a trade if the mover and at least one other player own a space without buildings. 1) The mover taps one of their now **pulsing** spaces (`tradePick`, `bk-pulse`) or cancels. 2) The other players each tap one of their pulsing spaces (`tradeOffer`) or pass (`tradePass`): one **90 second** clock for everybody (`TRADE_SEC`, `phaseClockMs` + `keepsClock`; at 0 the mover cancels / the others pass). 3) The mover sees the offers and takes one (`tradeAccept {from}`: the two spaces swap owners and **both get $100** from the bank, `tradeBonus`) or passes (`tradeCancel`). The trade panel is a pop-up in **the middle of the board** (`TradePanel`, `bk-trade-box`; the action area under the board just says to look there), so no space is ever covered and the pulsing spaces around it stay tappable; tapping a chip (or any space that does not pulse) opens its card. Computers never trade (the mover cancels, the others pass).
- **Money**: cash may go negative, **below -$500 a player is bankrupt**: out of the game, their spaces go back to the bank. The UI warns below $0. Selling a space (SELL on its card) gives its **flat sell price from the bank**: it never goes to auction and is free again; an owned space can only change hands through the trade on the Auction corner. It gives its **sell price (80 % of the price, rounded to 5)** plus half the cost of every building on it; a building sells for half its cost.
- **Net worth** (the green number) = cash + sell price of every space + half the cost of every building. Host options (lobby): net worth to win **3k / 5k / 7k / 10k / none**, rounds **20 / 30 / 45 / no limit**, auction unbought spaces **on / off**. The game ends when somebody reaches the target (richest one wins), when a round limit is passed (richest wins) or when one player is left. Standings: the winner, then by net worth, bankrupt players last (the later out the better).

## The board is data (`board.default.json`)

`config.ts` (`boardConfigSchema`, Zod) validates it; `DEFAULT_BOARD` is what the game uses (the board is copied into the state). Edit the JSON to add or remove spaces or colours. Rules the schema enforces: the first space is `start`, there is a `jail`, ids are unique, the number of spaces is a **multiple of 4** (a square ring: Start bottom-left, the ring runs up the left side and clockwise, so index N/4 is the top-left corner, N/2 top-right, 3N/4 bottom-right; `gridPos` in `apps/web/src/games/bankrupt/grid.ts`).

Tile kinds: `start`, `jail`, `auction`, `card`, `property` (`color`, `group`, `price`, `sell`, `rent[4]` = base / 1 / 2 / 3 buildings, `buildCost`), `transport` (the special spaces: `rent[]` by how many of them the owner has, no buildings). Money rules (`salary`, `jailFee`, `jailTurns`, `bankruptBelow`, `startCash`, `startCashStep`, `tradeBonus`) and the card deck sit next to the tiles.

The prices were read off the screenshots of the game this one is modelled on: Rio $100 (rent 10, buildings 30 / 90 / 160, build 50), Moscow $200 (30, 80 / 190 / 570, 120), Beijing $300 (40, 150 / 300 / 750, 170), Tokyo $420 (70, 200 / 500 / 900, 200), sell price 80 %. The other spaces were interpolated on price through those four points and written out as numbers. The prices of Railway and Electricity ($150) and the cards are our own guesses.

## State and moves

`BankruptState` is public (view = state): players (`cash`, `pos`, `jail`, `bankrupt`, `outAt`), `owned[tileId] = {owner, level}`, `phase` (`roll`, `buy`, `after`, `jailAsk`, `auction`, `trade`, `over`), `turn` / `round`, `sixes`, `dice` (one die), `auction`, `trade`, `events` (what the last move did, the screen replays it) and `seq`. Moves: `roll`, `buy`, `decline`, `payJail`, `wait`, `build`, `sellBuilding`, `sellProperty`, `bid`, `passBid`, `tradePick`, `tradeOffer`, `tradePass`, `tradeAccept`, `tradeCancel`. `getCurrentPlayers`: the mover, every bidder who is not the highest, or in a trade the mover / the players who have not answered. Computers (`autoMove`) buy when $150 stays in the bank, build on full sets with a $300 cushion, bid up to the value, pay jail when rich and sell when deep in debt. Errors are `BANK-1xx` (`errors.ts`).

## Screen (`BankruptGame.tsx`)

Normal `GameScreen` top bar (back, rules, chat, settings). Under it: the goal line ("$3,000 net worth to win · round 1/20"), the player tags (token, avatar, name, cash and the green net worth; tap for the tooltip bubble; a red cash number below 0), the board (`Board.tsx`, `data-testid` `bk-board`, spaces `bk-tile-<id>`, tokens `bk-token-<playerId>`, the owner's colour along the bottom of a space, buildings on the right), the big die (it tumbles while it rolls) and a line about the last move in the middle, then the action area (`bk-roll`, `bk-pay-jail`, `bk-wait-jail`, `bk-end` = Skip on the Auction corner, auction `bk-bid-1|2|5` / `bk-pass` / `bk-cancel-auction`). Tapping a space opens `TileSheet` (price / rent / sell price / building rows like the original; the owner can build, sell a building or the space, or auction it on the Auction corner). Tokens walk one space per `STEP_MS` after the dice have rolled (`ROLL_MS`); computers wait for it (`settleMs`). **Buy overlay** (`BuyOverlay.tsx`, `bk-buy-overlay`): when your token has stopped on a free space you can afford, a card pops up (if you cannot pay for it there is no card: a red "Not enough money for <space>" note (`bk-not-enough`) shows for a moment and the space is passed for you, so the game goes on at once) with the space, its price / rent / sell price and building rents, and **Buy** (`bk-buy`) or **Cancel** (`bk-decline`). **Money animation** (`useCashFx.ts`): every payment (rent, Start salary, a purchase, an auction sale) plays after the walk: the amount pops on the space, the payer's cash counts down, banknotes (`sprites/bankrupt/cash.svg`) fly to the receiver and the receiver's cash counts up (the green net worth follows); when you pass Start and then have to pay in the same move, the salary notes divide: the share of the payment flies on to the one you pay, the rest to your own cash (a payment bigger than the salary plays its left-over as a normal payment); reduced motion skips it. The tokens glide and hop from space to space (`bk-hop`).
Lobby options: `LobbyOptions.tsx` (`bk-target-<n>`, `bk-turns-<n>`, the auction switch `bk-auction-switch` with an (i) tooltip `bk-auction-info`). Rules sheet: `BankruptRules.tsx`.

## Luck cards on screen

A move that draws a luck card (`BankruptEvent` `card`, with `kind`: gain / pay / birthday / party / travel / repair / jail) pauses the game: the token stops on the card space, the card (`LuckCard.tsx`, the kit's card with a big pixel icon from `sprites/bankrupt/card-*.svg` and the text) slides in (`CARD_SLIDE_MS`), stays `CARD_SHOW_MS` (2 s) and slides away; **only then** the effect is shown (the token walks on for a `moveTo` card, jumps to jail, the payments fly: `pay` events, `useCashFx`). Nobody can act meanwhile (`settled`), and computers wait too (`settleMs`). Reduced motion: no slide, a shorter stay.

## Seats on the screen

I sit **bottom centre** (up to four players: everybody else on top, left to right = turn order) or **bottom left** (five or six players), the others follow clockwise round the board; with five or six the extra ones sit on the bottom row next to me (clockwise runs right to left along the bottom, so the player just before me is right next to me). A watcher sees the first seat bottom left.

## Waiting for animations

Nobody can roll (or pay jail / wait) while the previous player's token is still walking, the die tumbles, a luck card is on show or a payment is flying (`animating` in `BankruptGame.tsx`). Sea Battle also keeps the board shut until the opponent's shot has finished (`foeShot`); Yatzy, Karma, Last Card and Pet Match already waited (`settled`, reveal locks, `replaying`).

**Confirming trade picks**: tapping a pulsing space only selects it (it turns green); the panel asks "Offer / Trade <space>?" with **Confirm** (`bk-trade-confirm`) or **Change** (`bk-trade-change`). Taking one of the offers is confirmed the same way (Trade -> Confirm / Back).

**Who owns what**: an owned property's price band turns into a **rent band** in the owner's token colour (`bk-rent-<id>`, cream text, navy on yellow / orange) showing the rent to pay; there is no frame round it, so a bought property is told from a free one (which still shows its price in the group colour) at a glance. The four special spaces have no band and keep the coloured frame.

**Special spaces on the board** (`Board.tsx`, `bk-special-<id>`): the name of Harbor / Railway / Airport / Power sits at the **bottom** of the space (the kit's font4, drawn over the baked name; Electricity reads POWER like the kit), the top has a band like a property's: **free** = the price on a dark band, **bought** = the rent on a band in the owner's token colour (`bk-rent-<id>`). They have no frame any more.

## Pop-ups stay inside the board, and nothing is spoiled

- Every pop-up (the property card, the buy card, the luck card, a corner's text, the not-enough-money note) is drawn **inside the middle of the board** (`Board` `overlay`, `bk-overlay`; the cards fit that box: `useArtScale` with a height) and never covers a space. The middle holds the hall (86 art px wide), the big die (23 cqw) and the note, centred.
- The board is drawn **as it was before the move** until the move has been played out (`ui` / `seen` in `BankruptGame.tsx`: the token walks, the die rolls, the card shows, the payments fly): who owns the space it is walking to, buildings, jail, bankruptcy and the green numbers only change at the end. A token sent to jail **walks** there (by the shorter way round); tokens never jump.
- The Roll button (and everything else) is inert from the instant a move arrives (`fresh`) until its last animation is over, so there is no instant in between where it can be tapped.

- While a trade runs the hall, die and note shrink away (`Board` `clear`, `.bk-away`) and nothing shows under the board (`bk-actions` is hidden). `TradePanel.tsx` + `MiniCard.tsx` (read-only miniature of a space's card).
- **Pick**: the mover sees "Choose property", the miniature of the glowing space they tapped, and **Pass** (`bk-trade-cancel`) / **Confirm** (`bk-trade-confirm`, greyed until a pick). The others see "<name> is picking…" with their own Pass (`tradePass`, now also allowed in this stage: it means "I do not want to trade"; when every other player has passed the trade is cancelled) and a greyed Confirm.
- **Offers**: the others see the mover's miniature, the swap arrow and their own pick (tap a glowing space, Confirm) or pass; the mover sees their own card and one box per player: the miniature of what was confirmed, an X for a pass, dots while choosing.

## Rainbow Road (lobby switch `rainbowRoad`, default off)

- When on (`BankruptOptions.rainbowRoad`, the "Rainbow Road" switch next to the auction one in the lobby), a mover who lands on a **free property of a street of exactly three** whose other two spaces belong to **two different players (neither is the mover)** gets a **Rainbow Road** button on the buy card instead of Buy (the X still passes / auctions). `rainbowRoad(state, id)` in `rules.ts` decides; it needs 3 players alive by definition.
- The move `{type:'rainbow'}` sells the two owned spaces back to the bank: each owner gets the space's sell price (`rainbow` event with `payouts`), the landed space stays free, the mover gets nothing (change in `applyMove` if the mover should be paid too). Error BANK-133.
- UI: `RainbowRoad.tsx` (seven-band rainbow wipes over the middle, the three cards sparkle, "Rainbow Road!"); the payouts go through the normal cash animation (`useCashFx`). The "not enough money" auto-pass does not apply while the road is on offer. Computers never use it.

- **Cha-ching** (`play('cash')`, synthesized in `lib/sound.ts`): plays for every payment of the cash show (`useCashFx`): rent, salary, buying, selling, cards, jail fee, trades, Rainbow Road, buildings (also with reduced motion, which skips the show but not the sound). A building bought or sold (`build` event, `amount`: cost, negative when sold) or a sold space is a short `quick` show that never holds the game up.

- **Moves are shown one at a time** (`BankruptGame.tsx`): a state that arrives while the last move is still being played out (a computer answers fast) waits in a queue and is shown when the walk, card and payments are done, so a token never stops short or lets the next player hop first. Three or more waiting: only the latest is kept.

- **Jail**: the cell door sound (`play('jail')`) plays when a token has been sent to jail and has arrived there (never for just passing the Jail space). When the player cannot pay the fee there is nothing to ask: no Pay / Pass, the turn just ends (`jailChoice` in `game.ts`).

## Auction, turn based (in the middle of the board)

- `Auction` has `turn` (whose turn it is) and `bids` (each player's highest bid). One player is up at a time, in seat order after the one who passed (the mover is last); only they may `bid` or `passBid` ("Not your turn" otherwise). A bid or a pass moves the turn to the next player who may still bid (the leader never gets the turn; someone who cannot afford to top the highest bid steps out by themselves). The 15 s clock (`phaseClockMs`) restarts with every move and when it runs out the player who is up steps out (`timeoutMove`).
- UI: `AuctionPanel.tsx` fills the middle of the board (hall and die shrink away, nothing under the board): the leader highlighted in gold on top with their amount, the other players under them with their bid, "PASSED" (greyed) or "bidding…" for the one who is up, the three quick bids (`bk-bid-1|2|5`) and Pass (`bk-pass`), enabled only on your turn.
- **Rainbow Road by auction**: when a space is not taken as a Rainbow Road, goes to auction and the winner ends up as the third different owner of the street, the street is sold anyway (all three owners are paid the sell price, the winner included).
- The Rainbow Road button is part of the card art (`button-rainbow.svg`, made with `.claude/skills/pogo-pixel-art/examples/bk_rainbow_button.py`).
- The board is as wide as the screen (up to the app column) and centred on it (`Board.tsx`), and the game screen never scrolls sideways.

- **Auto roll** (`bk-roll-lock`, the padlock next to the Roll button, also next to "Waiting for …" so it can be unlocked on somebody else's turn; like the lobby's Ready lock): while locked, my turn rolls by itself (0.5 s after the last move has been played out), turn after turn, and stops by itself whenever a choice is needed (buy / Rainbow Road, jail question, auction, trade), because those are not the roll phase. Local to the screen, off when a game starts.

The screen below it is the stage: the board keeps its proportions and is **as wide as the screen** (98.5 % of the width, its drop shadow included, not on the whole-pixel grid) and sits in the **vertical middle**; my card and the Roll button (`roll-the-dice-sm`, 187 x 48) share **one row in the bottom fifth**; the extra opponents of a 5 or 6 player game stand in a row above it; the goal label and the top row (up to 3 opponents) are at the top. The art scale is a whole number of device pixels per art pixel when that fills the width (phones), else it follows the width (`useStage.ts`).
- **The log** (`bk-note`) keeps the last **three turns**: one entry per player, the name once (`Robo: rolled 4 · bought Berlin · paid Banker $25 rent`), moves of the same player in a row are joined; newest on top at 100 %, then 90 %, 80 %.
- **Sounds**: `play('tap')` for every click that has no sound of its own (Roll, bids, pass, opening / closing a space, the padlock, tapping a space to trade); money moves make the cash sound instead. Pop-ups (buy card, space card) close by shrinking and fading (`useCloseAnim`, `.bk-pop-out`).
- A frog in jail is chained up with a padlock (`frog/jail-chains.svg`, `FrogToken` `jailed`).
- Settings: Admin panel (developers) at the top above Room, a blank line under CRT, Default bottom left, Exit bottom right, "Not <name>? Switch player" centred and smaller at the bottom; the Close X is centred on the title row (`Sheet` `headerClass`).

- **A roll that lands on the Jail space** (one die: the frog always starts on the top row, e.g. on Jakarta with a 6): the frog does not hop, once the die has settled the car comes and fetches it from where it stands (`plan.trigger` `roll`). Also, when a **luck card sends a frog to jail from the top row** (the "?" corner or the spaces next to it: the car only drives along the top row) the frog does not hop there: after the card has slid away `runPolice` (in `useWalking`, `BankruptGame.tsx`) plays the artist's sequence in a **fast version** (`POLICE` timings, about 2 times quicker than the pack: the fast version played at 80 % of its speed): the car comes out of the Jail space with its siren flashing (a / b every ~140 ms), drives to the frog, U-turns, takes it (the frog's token is hidden, it shows in the back window, 1 px bump), drives back, delivers it (the Jail space shows it behind the bars), leaves to the right. The screen counts as busy until the car has gone. A card from anywhere else than the top row keeps the old walk; reduced motion skips the car.

- **The repair-bill luck card** (pay $ per building) never bankrupts: it only takes what the debt limit (`bankruptBelow`) still allows, so cash ends at the limit at worst.

- **Rainbow Road, every way a space changes hands** (`rainbowCheck` in `game.ts`): a plain **buy**, an **auction win** and a **trade** that leave a street of exactly three with three different owners sell the street (everybody is paid the sell price). **Computers take the road** when it is offered (`autoMove` plays `rainbow` instead of buying), so a bot never ends up as the third owner without a sale.
- **Auto roll and debt**: when I am below $0 the lock may roll **once** after it is switched on; after that roll (the move played out) it switches itself off and the middle shows "You are below $0. Auto roll is off." for 2 s, fading out over the last 0.3 s (`bk-debt-warning`, `debtWarn` show / fade). The timers live in a ref (switching the lock off re-runs the effect, which used to cancel the timer and leave the warning stuck). Switching it on again allows one more roll.

- **Frog shadow**: every frog (all colours, all four directions, in jail) stands on a soft pixel-ellipse ground shadow (`frog/shadow-ground.svg`, 25 x 9 art px, made with `bk_frog_shadow.py`: darker core, lighter rim), 3 px wider than the frog on both sides and showing 3 px below its feet. During a hop it stays on the ground and, in step with the lift table (`HOP.lift`), shrinks to 68 % and fades to 60 % at the highest point and grows back as the frog lands (`SHADOW_APEX` in `frog.ts`, one Web Animations curve in `FrogToken.tsx`, no switching between two pictures).
- **Whose turn it is** ("Waiting for <name>", the debt warning; `bk-wait`, `bk-warning` in `bk-actions`) sits **above** my card / Roll row (above the extra-seats row when there is one), not under it.

## Free Froggo (lobby switch `freeFroggo`, default off)

- When on, a jailed frog whose turn comes up (`settleTurn` in `game.ts`, instead of just sitting the turn out) starts a **vote**: phase `bail`, `state.bail = {subject, amount: FREE_FROGGO_BAIL (50), voters, accepted}`. Every other living player is asked to pay the bail (`bailPay` / `bailRefuse`, simultaneous like a trade, `getCurrentPlayers` = the voters who have not said yes). **Only when ALL say yes** does each pay 50 to the bank (`bail` event, cash show to the town hall), the frog's jail is 0 (`free` event) and it plays its turn (phase `roll`). **One no** ends the vote at once (`bailRefused` event, nobody pays) and the jailed turn is sat out as usual. The vote is asked **once per stay in jail** (`bailAsked`, reset when a frog is sent to jail again) and is not started when somebody cannot afford 50 or the switch is off. Computers always pay (`autoMove`); a human who does not answer in `BAIL_SEC` (15 s, `phaseClockMs` / `timeoutMove`) says no. Errors BANK-134 / BANK-135.
- UI: `BailPanel.tsx` in the middle of the board (who has paid, the Free froggo! / No buttons, the clock); log lines for `bail` / `bailRefused`; the chains come off when the jail counter hits 0.

## Auction: bid step and the sold show

- **Bid buttons** (`bidStep(price)` in `game.ts`, used by `AuctionPanel` and the computers): the left button raises by $5 on the cheap spaces up to $20 on the dearest (price / 22.5 rounded to 5, between 5 and 20); the other two are 2 x and 5 x that. The first bid on a space is still its opening bid.
- **Sold show** (`AuctionWon.tsx`, `AUCTION_WON_MS` 2.7 s): when a space is won the middle of the board shows the gavel (`assets/sprites/bankrupt/auction/gavel.svg`, `block.svg`, made with `bk_gavel.py`) coming down on its block with the knock (`play('hammer')` in `lib/sound.ts`), "SOLD!", the winner's name and "wins <space> for $X", and the space's card flies towards the winner's seat and shrinks away (`bk-gavel`, `bk-block`, `bk-sold`, `bk-win-card` in `index.css`). The payment to the town hall and the new owner on the board follow after it (`winOn` in `BankruptGame.tsx` holds `useCashFx` and the board).

- Light comes from the top left everywhere in the app, so every shadow falls to the **bottom right** (the board's drop shadow, the frogs' ground shadow). Keep it that way for new art (also in `CLAUDE.md`).

- **Computers never trade, and a trade with nobody is not even opened**: `BankruptPlayer.bot` (from `GamePlayer.bot`, which the room sets) takes computers out of `traders()`; when the mover has nothing to swap, or the only players with spaces are computers (or the mover is a computer), the Auction corner opens no pick at all (`tradeNone` event). When a trade ends without a deal (all passed, the mover cancelled: `tradeEnd`) it ends at once. Both play a short "No trade / Trade cancelled" show in the middle of the board (`TradeCancelled.tsx`, `TRADE_CANCELLED_MS` 1.9 s, red cross stamping over two empty card spots; `noDealOn` in `BankruptGame.tsx` holds the game for it).

## The wheel and the town plaza (`board_inside_wheel.zip`)

- **The inside of the board** is the artist's town plaza (`assets/sprites/bankrupt/plaza/`: `board-inside-plaza.svg` 193 x 193 starting at (32, 32) of the board, fountain frames, cloud shadows, birds, Pogo's hub, the pointer). `Plaza.tsx` draws it on one canvas (4 px per art px, scaled by the page): fountain 140 ms, two cloud shadows, the wheel and its shadow, the pointer, birds; reduced motion draws it still. **Pogo's hub is a real image (`bk-hall`) on top: all the money flies to and from it** (the town hall is gone).
- **The wheel is the die.** `wheel.ts` (port of the artist's `wheel.js`) redraws the wheel pixel by pixel at its angle; it spins onto the number the game rolled (`spinToIndex`, `ROLL_MS` 3000 = about 2.6 s turning + a short flash; a tick sound per slice edge, `play('tick')`). Tap the wheel to roll when you can. Not done: dragging the wheel by hand, a mute toggle for the ticks (the app's Sounds switch covers it).
- **Special wheel** (lobby switch `specialWheel`, **on by default**): after **three 6s in a row the 4th roll is the special wheel** (`SIXES_FOR_WHEEL`), and the new luck card **"Fortunate wheel!"** (`effect: wheel`, sets `BankruptPlayer.wheel`) makes your next roll one too. Its slices are `SPECIAL_SLICES` = `1 2 $50 3 4 5 6 JAIL 1 2 3 4` (the artist's `X2` is left out for now; it is for later). The game picks the slice (`rng.int(12)`, event `roll.wheel = {idx, slice, from}`): a number moves as usual, **$50** pays from the bank (cash show) and the turn ends, **JAIL** sends the frog to jail (the police car comes from the top row, as for a card). While it stands still the wheel shows the set of the NEXT roll. Switched off, the wheel card is not in the pack and the sixes work as before (an extra roll each).
- The roll messages sit under the wheel (board y 174), over the fountain.

- **The luck cards are a real deck** (`BankruptState.deck`, `cardPack`, `shuffled` in `game.ts`): the pack is shuffled at the start (11 cards, 10 without the Special wheel option), cards are dealt from the top (a card cannot come twice before all the others have), and when the deck is empty it is shuffled again. The order of the cards to come is hidden from the clients (`getPlayerView`).
