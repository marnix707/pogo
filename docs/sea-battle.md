## Sea Battle (the classic ships game; packages/shared/src/games/seabattle, apps/web/src/games/seabattle)

- Id `seabattle`, name **Sea Battle** (the artist's logo says so; "Battleship" is a trademark). Two players (a person and/or a computer), 10 x 10 sea, the classic fleet (carrier 5, battleship 4, cruiser 3, submarine 3, destroyer 2; `FLEET`, ships may touch but not overlap), a miss passes the turn, **a hit (a sunk ship too) gives another shot** (`applyMove` keeps `turn` with the shooter; the computer's `autoMove` reports `endsTurn: false` after a hit). First to sink all 17 squares wins.
- Logic: `fleet.ts` (`Ship {kind,x,y,vertical}`, `shipCells`, `validFleet`, `overlaps`, `randomFleet(rng)`), `game.ts`. Phases `place` (both players at once; `keepsClock`: one deadline for the whole phase; the move `{type:'ready', ships}` is sent once, **placing itself happens on the device** and the server only validates the fleet), `play` (`{type:'fire', x, y}`), `over`. `first` (who starts) is drawn from the Rng when the game is created; the clients animate it as a coin flip. `getPlayerView`: my ships, shots at me (`incoming`), my shots (`outgoing`), the enemy ships I have sunk (`sunk`, full detail), and **only when the game is over `revealed`**: the enemy ships still afloat (the loser sees the winner's survivors). The opponent's fleet never leaves the server before that. `lastEvent {seq, by, x, y, result: miss|hit|sunk, sunk?, win}`. `COIN_FLIP_MS` (3.6 s) is `clockBonusMs`/`settleMs` before the first shot, `SHOT_FX_MS` (1.5 s) `settleMs` after each shot, so computers wait for the animations. Computers (`supportsBots`): random fleet, then hunt on a chessboard pattern and finish ships they have hit (`autoMove`). Errors SEA-101..105, SEA-125. Tests: `game.test.ts` (40 auto-played games).
- UI: `SeaBattleGame.tsx` (placing, playing, coin, radar), `Board.tsx` (a 10 x 10 sea drawn with the artist's animated board `assets/sprites/battle/board/board-animated.svg` (161 art px, loops its own waves; `board-00.svg` with reduced motion; `board-labelled-*` with A-J / 1-10 labels is unused), ships, hit/miss markers, burning flames on hits, the aim reticle, the bomb fx, the radar; every square is a `sb-cell-x-y` button when it is my turn), `Placement.tsx` (`PlacementBoard`: draws the **still** sea (`Board still`: the animated sea made dragging stutter on phones; a dragged ship also moves in a local render and is committed on drop; the flame / reticle timers only run when there is something to animate), **drag** a ship to move it, **tap** it to turn it, an overlapping drop jumps back; `Random` button `sb-random` next to `Play` `sb-ready`; `deviceRng`), `CoinFlip.tsx` (the coin toss with **the players' pictures instead of heads and tails**, `sb-coin`), `art.ts` (the artist's pixel art in `assets/sprites/battle/`: `ships/` h/v/sunk, `tiles/` water, `markers/`, `fx/` bomb, explosion, splash; 1 cell = 16 art px shown at 2x = 32 px, the small own board at 1x; pictures are preloaded when the app starts). **Screen structure** (like the reference game): a players header (my picture and name, **between them a small arrow `◀`/`▶` pointing at whoever is up, right next to the seconds left (both only when there is a turn timer; with the timer Off the middle is empty) (`◀ 23` when I am up, `23 ▶` for the opponent)** (`sb-turn-arrow`, `sb-countdown`; **VS** while placing and at the end), the opponent's; the one who is up has a gold ring and a small draining clock bar, `sb-player-me|opponent`, `data-active`; "✓ Ready" while placing), the big enemy sea, the message under it (`sb-event`: what just happened, `sb-status`: whose turn / what to do), a row with the five enemy ships still to sink (`sb-enemy-fleet`: a sunk one shows as a wreck, `data-sunk`), my own small sea centred below **Spammable**: after a hit it is still your turn, and the board only stays shut for the moment the server needs to answer your shot (`inflight`, until `shotCount` changes), not for the animation, so you can fire again while the bomb is still falling; several shots can animate at once (`fxs`, one `BombFx` per `seq`; sinkings are queued per shot in `pendingSinks`). Aiming: **one tap on a square fires** (no Fire button, no second tap; the reticle sits on the square while the bomb is on its way; the clock is the small bar under the active player's picture, drawn only when the room has a turn timer: every game starts with the timer Off). A shot plays the bomb (4 frames), then blast or splash, and only then does the marker appear (`hideShot`); the opponent's shots play on my small board. Sounds `whistle`, `boom`, `splash`, `ping` in `lib/sound.ts`.
- **Sinking** (`SinkFx` in `Board.tsx`, `.ship-sinking` / `.sunk-pop` in `index.css`, sound `sink`): when a shot sinks a ship, after its bomb has landed a chain of blasts runs along the ship's squares, the ship shakes and darkens, a "SUNK! <ship>" banner pops over that sea (mine or the enemy's), and then the charred wreck takes its place (`SINK_MS` 0.9 s, a quick chain of blasts 40 ms apart, locked for input; the server adds it to `settleMs`). **Coin flip**: the coin spins up and slows down, stops at the top and shows who starts (no fall back down; `COIN_FLIP_MS` 2.8 s). **Placing**: tapping a ship turns it around its middle, dragging moves it (pointer capture is on the layer that owns the handlers). **Shots are fast**: the bomb falls in 4 frames of 45 ms, the blast / splash plays at 40 ms a frame (`BOMB_FRAME_MS`, `BLAST_FRAME_MS`; `SHOT_FX_MS` 0.8 s), drawn at the art's own size (2 x 2 squares for the bomb and blast, the burning flame 1 x 1: bigger overlapped the neighbours). **Game over** has two sequences that end together: the **winner** sees the last bomb and the last ship sinking, then the standings open with "You won!" popping up in the middle and gliding to the top (no radar: nothing was left to find); the **loser** skips the sinking of their last ship and goes straight from the bomb to the **radar** (event-driven: `fxDone` calls `startRadar`, `RADAR_START_MS` 0.2 s later it begins; the unfound ships are not drawn before that). The radar is a green overlay sweeps the enemy sea (`Radar` in `Board.tsx`, CSS `.radar-*` in `index.css`: tint, rings, a rotating beam; the unfound ships sit in a layer that a **conic mask** (`.radar-reveal`, `@property --radar-a` animated 0 to 360 degrees in step with the beam) opens clockwise from the top, so a ship shows exactly as far as the beam has swept over it: at 50 % across a ship only that half is visible), then the standings after `resultsDelayMs`: the loser (whose view carries `revealed` ships) waits `RADAR_END_MS` = last bomb 0.8 s + `RADAR_START_MS` 0.2 s + the sweep `RADAR_MS` 2.2 s (a full 360 degrees) + `RADAR_HOLD_MS` 0.5 s with the finished radar on screen; the winner (nothing to find) `END_MS` 2.6 s. Reduced motion: the static radar without the sweep. E2E: `e2e/tests/seabattle.spec.ts` (a whole game against a computer, placing by dragging, a screenshot run with real motion).
