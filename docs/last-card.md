## Last Card (the UNO-style colour-match game; packages/shared/src/games/lastcard, apps/web/src/games/lastcard)

- Standard rules, 108 cards (`cards.ts`: id 0-99 = four colours x 25 (one 0, two each 1-9, two each skip / reverse / draw two), 100-103 wild, 104-107 wild draw four; `cardInfo`, `canPlayCard`, `cardPoints`), 2-6 players, 7 cards each, the game starts on a number card (action cards are buried), random first player. Play a card of the current colour or the same number/symbol; wilds always, **including Wild Draw Four (no "only without the colour" restriction)**. Skip skips, reverse flips `direction` (with two players it is a skip). **Draw penalties stack**: a Draw Two / Wild Draw Four does not make the next player draw at once, it adds 2 / 4 to `state.pending` (`view.pending`, `event.pending`) and the turn passes to the next player, who may only answer with another Draw Two or Wild Draw Four (either on either, any colour: `canPlayCard(..., pending)`) or `draw` the whole total (one `draw` move: `event.drew` = the total, `event.skipped` = themselves, the turn then moves on). The UI shows "+N waiting" in the event line and the draw button reads "Draw N cards". Nothing to play: `draw` one card; if it fits it is `state.drawn` and may be played or `pass`ed, otherwise the turn ends by itself. The discard pile is reshuffled into the deck when it runs out. First out wins (`state.winner`); the others are ranked by the points in their hand (number value, action 20, wild 50), ties share a rank; scores are hidden (`hideScores`).
- **Last Card! and call-outs** (the reference site's rule, now played as a speed race, see the button below): call UNO! **right before** (`uno: true` on the `play` that leaves one card; the button can be _armed_ with two cards) or **right after** (`{type:'uno'}`) playing the second-to-last card. A `play` that leaves one card without the flag opens `state.callWindow = playerId`; the window closes on that player's `uno`, on a `callout`, or on the **first draw / pass / play by anybody** ("the next player started"): then nobody is punished (`uno` after that is refused, "Too late"). While it is open **any other player** may send `{type:'callout', target}`: the target draws **4** cards (`event.kind 'callout'`, `victim`, `state.catchCall {seq, byId, targetId}` kept apart from `lastEvent` like `unoCall`); both moves are allowed out of turn and are _free moves_ (no clock restart). **You cannot finish with a special card** (action or wild): `validateMove` refuses it, you must draw. Computers always call UNO! with the play, and `autoMove` calls out a forgetful player 85 % of the time (`CALLOUT_CHANCE`, `endsTurn: false`); `settleMs` is 2.2 s while a window is open, so computers never act before a human could press UNO! or catch somebody. The client additionally **holds** everybody (cards, draw, pile; `CALL_HOLD_MS` 1.5 s, local) when somebody else is in the window. A 300-game auto-play test guards against endless games and lost cards.
- **Special cards** (the artist's `shield.svg`, `blue-ghost.svg`, `red-skull.svg` in `assets/sprites/colour-cards/`; ids 108-109 shield, 110-111 ghost, 112-113 skull, `DECK_SIZE` 114, kinds `shield|ghost|skull` in `cards.ts`): the **Shield** (colourless, never asks for a colour) can only be played against a waiting +2/+4 and sends the whole stack **back** to whoever played the last draw card (`state.pendingFrom`, `event.reflectedTo`; the colour in force stays; that player may answer with a draw card or a shield again, or draw it; error LAST-109 otherwise). The **Blue Ghost** (colour blue; plays on blue or another ghost) makes every other player holding **fewer** cards than the ghost player has left draw 2; the **Red Skull** (red; on red or another skull) makes every other player **without a red card** draw 2 (wilds are not red). They are played like any card (no skip, the turn passes on) and cannot be played while a penalty waits; none of the three can finish the game. Victims are listed in `event.victims` (`punish()` in `game.ts`), `settleMs` adds 1.4 s after them so computers wait for the show; computers prefer the shield as the answer to a penalty. UI: event line texts, cards fly to every victim, a banner `special-banner` ("SHIELD!" / "BOO!" / "SKULL!", `data-kind`) and sounds `shield` / `ghost` / `skull`, rules text in `LastCardRules`.
- State/view: `LastCardState` (players with `called`, `stock`, `pile`, `color`, `turn`, `direction`, `drawn`, `winner`, `lastEvent`, `unoCall {seq, playerId}`: the latest call is kept apart from `lastEvent` so the next move cannot hide the announcement). `LastCardView` shows my hand, the others' counts and `called`, the top card, up to three cards below it, `mustCall` (I hold one card and have not called).
- Web (`apps/web/src/games/lastcard`): `LastCardGame.tsx` (seats clockwise from me with a yellow background for the player on turn and a red UNO! badge / pulsing "1 card…" for a single card, draw pile + discard pile on a ring in the current colour with the direction arrow, event line `lastcard-event`, my hand fanned to fit (scrolls when huge), Draw / Pass button carrying the turn clock, the **Last Card! button** (`uno-button`, a speed race): hidden by default; while somebody sits on one card without having called (`view.callWindow`) it pulses in the middle above the hand for **everybody**: the player with one card sees "Last Card!" (`data-state="urgent"`, sends `uno`), the others "Call out!" (`data-state="callout"`, sends `callout` on that player); whoever is first wins (the server decides), a caught player draws 4. The old arming with two cards and the per-seat Call out button are gone (the `uno: true` flag on `play` stays for computers), wild colour picker `lastcard-color-picker`, the **announcement** `uno-called` (big "UNO!" + who called it, 1.9 s, for everybody, not for a call made before joining; keyframes `lastcard-call` in `index.css`, fade only with reduced motion)). Card controls are Karma's: the first tap on a playable card selects (lifts) it, tapping the selected card again plays it (a wild then asks for the colour); tapping anything that is not a button, an unplayable card, a new move or the turn passing drops the selection (`data-selected`); unplayable cards are dimmed and explain why in the event line. Test ids: `lastcard-game`, `lastcard-card-<id>` with `data-playable`, `lastcard-top`, `lastcard-draw`, `lastcard-draw-button`, `lastcard-pass`, `lastcard-seat-<id>`, `lastcard-color`, `lastcard-direction`. E2E: `e2e/tests/lastcard.spec.ts` plays a whole game against two computers.
- **Card sizes and hand** = Karma's: `useCardScale(LASTCARD_CHROME)` (`lib/cardScale.ts`, chrome 300px; Karma uses 238) gives `myFont = 18 * scale`, `pileFont = 19.5 * scale`, `stockFont = 14.5 * scale`, and my hand is laid out by `handLayout()` (`lib/handLayout.ts`, shared with Karma: 10-30 % overlap, shrinks to 75 % before another row, extra rows overlap by half a card, `data-rows`). Overlapped cards intercept a centre click: tests tap the left strip (`STRIP` from `karma-helpers.ts`).
- **Card animations** are Karma's: the FLIP slide lives in `lib/cardFlip.ts` (`useCardFlip`, `CardFlipContext`, `resetFlipBoxes`, `boxOf`; `PlayingCard` and `ColourCard` both use it, `PlayingCard` re-exports the old names). In `LastCardGame`: my hand cards slide when the hand re-fans and grow out of the draw pile when dealt to me (stagger 130 ms; `handOrigin`/`handDelay`), a played card slides from my hand to the pile, an opponent's played card flies in from their seat (`pileOrigin`), and cards an opponent draws (a draw, Draw Two/Four, a call-out) fly as face-down cards from the deck to their seat (`FlyingBack`). Off for the first paint and with reduced motion. **Rotation arrows** (`DirectionRing.tsx`, the artist's `assets/sprites/direction/`: `direction-cw|ccw.svg`, `frames/cw-00..07`, `ccw-00..07`, ring 77x93 art px around the 45x61 card at (16,16)): when a move reverses the direction (`event.reversed`) the ring flashes around the discard pile in the NEW direction: one spin turn (8 frames x 120 ms, half speed), the static ring 600 ms, a 400 ms fade (reduced motion: the static ring for 1 s). The img needs `maxWidth: none` (Tailwind's `img { max-width: 100% }` squeezes it into the card width). `data-testid="direction-ring"`.
- **Card art**: the artist's colour-match deck (`assets/sprites/colour-cards/`: `{red,yellow,green,blue}-{0..9,skip,reverse,draw2}.svg`, `wild.svg`, `wild-draw4.svg`, `back.svg`, `deck.json`; 45x61 art px, colours red `#fd5f55`, yellow `#fda200`, green `#56a786`, blue `#3aaef7` = `CARD_COLORS` in `ColourCard.tsx`). `cardArt.ts` picks every file up by name with `import.meta.glob`, so redrawn art only needs the file replaced. The game is called **Last Card** everywhere players see it (the id is `lastcard`); the call is "Last Card!" in the UI too (button, announcement `uno-called`, badge); only the internal names stay "uno" ( `uno-button` / `uno-called` / `uno-badge-*` / `uno-risk-*` test ids, `unoCall` / `uno` fields, moves `uno` and `callout`). Game picker logo: `GameUi.logo` (`assets/sprites/brand/logos/{yatzy,karma,last-card}.svg`, shown on the Home game cards instead of the emoji).
